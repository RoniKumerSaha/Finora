Finora logo tile background — animated variants

Each card shows the 60×60 logo tile sitting on Finora's app background (#1E2A26). Pick which animation (if any) you want on the logo's black bg, and I'll wire it into Shell.tsx.

1 · Solid app bg, no animation
Reference. Tile is var(--bg) with no animation — same color as the surrounding Shell, so it visually dissolves into the sidebar.
2 · Solid bg + full hue rotation (8s)
Lively but subtle. Tile stays the same base color; hue-rotate cycles the spectrum slowly so the bg shimmers between cool blue, green, and warm gold — same trick we just used on the wordmark.
3 · Radial glow cycling brand colors
Softest. A glow pulses from center outward, cycling through primary → info → accent. Reads as "the logo is glowing" without moving.
4 · Spinning conic gradient
Most energetic. Full conic rainbow spins behind the bars. The bars themselves turn white so they pop against the spinning color. Might be too loud for a brand mark.
5 · Linear sweep (highlight travels across)
Premium feel. A soft highlight glides diagonally across the tile every 4s — like light catching the edge of a glossy logo. Tile still reads as bg-colored most of the time.
6 · Subtle hue sway (±25°, slower)
My pick. Tile bg barely shifts — a gentle ±25° hue sway over 10s. Same family as #2 but stays within "near-neutral with a hint of warmth" so it never looks like a Christmas tree.
7 · Solid bg + bars pulse in unison (1.4s)
Three bars (green · blue · gold) all scale together from short to tall and back. Reads as a heartbeat — calm, rhythmic, unmistakably alive.
8 · Solid bg + bars wave (1.2s, offset)
Each bar scales on its own delay, so the motion rolls left-to-right like an audio equalizer. More energetic than #7 — clearly "this thing is doing something".
9 · Subtle hue sway + bar wave (combined)
Most polished. Combines your preferred bg treatment (#6) with the equalizer bars (#8). Tile sways gently while the bars ripple — both motions stay subtle so they don't fight.
