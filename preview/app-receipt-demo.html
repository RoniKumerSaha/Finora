Finora — the whole app, as receipts

Every card in Finora becomes a piece of paper. The goal card is a till receipt. The investment card is a bank certificate — engraved terms, a serial number, a rate seal, a tear along the edge. This demo applies that same document grammar to every page: Home, Transactions, Accounts, Goals, Investments, Debts, Plan, Insight and Settings.

Three shells, one grammar — sheet (a plain receipt), cert (an instrument, tinted by its own tone), statement (a rolled-up summary). Every shell shares the same perforation, the same dashed rules, the same line items, the same tilted stamp.
