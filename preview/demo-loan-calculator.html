Loan Calculator — a projection, not a debt

A loan projection wears the same debt certificate as a real debt — the same perforated sheet, the same engraved fields, the same rail. What marks it as a projection is the dashed PLANNED stamp standing in for the APR seal: there is no instrument behind it yet. The tone is the danger ink a loan always carries.
