Plan my month — the workbench

The Month Planner is a workbench, not a document. There's no certificate shell here — just a grid of editable cards, a preset picker that fills the grid for you, and two floating editors for bulk work. What it keeps from the certificate family is the perforated edge on the panel that holds the grid, so the paper metaphor survives.
