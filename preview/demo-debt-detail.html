Debt detail — the IOU, torn along the edge

A debt is the oldest document in the app: an IOU slip. The certificate carries the counterparty's name, the amount, the rate seal and a tear-off stub down the left edge — the copy they keep. Editing rewrites the terms on the slip; the activity ledger on the right is the running account of every payment against it.
