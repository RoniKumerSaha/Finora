Transactions — one long ledger sheet

Transactions are the one place where a card has to hold a hundred rows, so the list stays a single sheet rather than a stack of cards — but it earns the same perforation, the same ruled header and the same day separators as every other document. Filters live above the sheet as chips; the running totals at the top of the sheet tell you what the month actually adds up to.
