Investment Planner — a promise, not a contract

The planner is the certificate family's planned branch. A plan wears the same investment certificate shell as a real holding — but the rate seal becomes a dashed PLANNED stamp, because a plan is a promise and there is no instrument behind it. Nothing here moves real money.
