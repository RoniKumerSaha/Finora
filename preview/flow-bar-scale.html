Flow bar — P2 with a bar scale

The base is P2 (bright axis, soft halo, bars anchored flush to the zero line). What is missing is a scale — right now the bars are lengths with nothing to measure them against, so "half the width" is a guess, not a number.

Below are five ways to put a scale on that column. S1 is the plain ruler, S2 adds numbers, S3 uses a fading gradient instead of hard ticks, S4 brackets the range, and S5 lifts the scale out of the rows into one shared band above them.

1The axis is nearly invisible. It is drawn in --divider (#343F3A), barely a shade above the card surface — so the column has no visible spine and the bars look like they float.
2The bars do not touch the axis. A 1px axis with bars starting at exactly 50% leaves a hairline of background between them, so the bars read as detached objects rather than bars growing out of a zero line.
3There is no scale. A bar's length is proportional to the largest net in the column — but nothing on screen says what that largest value IS, so the reader can see the SHAPE of the year but not judge any single bar.
4A tiny overspend vanishes. October's ৳1,118 overspend is a 3px stub against a ৳6,11,000 scale.
