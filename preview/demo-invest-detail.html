Investment detail — the bank certificate, and its maturity

An investment is a bank certificate: engraved terms, a serial number, a rate seal and a maturity date. What the old detail screen lacked was a sense of time — so the certificate now carries a maturity rail, the numbers move from "what I put in" to "what I'll get out", and the activity ledger tracks every contribution and payout.
