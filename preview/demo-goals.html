Goals — the receipt grid

A goal is a till receipt: plain paper, a dashed rule, a running total and a stamp. There is no progress bar and no percent pill — the receipt is the record, and its numbers carry the progress. Target / Saved / Progress / Due read as a statement, and Still needed is the receipt's total.
