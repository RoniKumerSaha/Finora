Investments — the certificate stack

An investment is a bond certificate: a perforated instrument carrying engraved terms, a serial number, a tilted rate seal, a two-line deposit ledger and the two values separated by a named interest figure. The Summary is pinned to the right at large widths, so the totals stay visible while you scroll the list.

A DPS pays a fixed installment every month for years, so its full deposit ledger can run to dozens of rows; an FDR has exactly one deposit. Both get the same two-line ledger — LAST DEPOSIT and NEXT DUE — so a 34-deposit DPS and a 1-deposit FDR are the same card, the same height.
