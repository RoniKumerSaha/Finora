The add screens — three doors, four forms, one language

Creating a record is a different job from reading one. The certificate family covers what you hold; these screens cover what you make — and a creation screen has to do two things at once: give you a form to fill in, and show you the document the form is producing.

So every screen here follows the same two-part grammar: a ruled form on the left (the document's blank lines), and a live certificate beside it that re-engraves itself as you type. You see the instrument before you commit to it.

1 · The type picker — three doors
What happened?
Pick one to continue.
What changed: the old picker was three plain cards with a label and a hint. These carry an example — because the real question isn't "income or expense?" (the colours already answer that) but "which of my entries is this?" The example is the part that was missing.
Why the examples
The three questions people actually ask
"Did I get paid?" Income
"Did I spend it?" Expense
"Did I just move it?" Transfer
A transfer is the one that gets mis-picked, because "I withdrew cash from the ATM" reads like an expense but is a transfer. Naming that example on the Transfer door is the single highest-value line on this screen.
The doors, and what they do to your money
