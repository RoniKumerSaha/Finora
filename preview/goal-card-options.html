Goals — nine ways to draw a promise

The Goals screen today renders each goal as a soft blue wash card: a star tile, the name, a green percentage pill, a progress bar, the saved/target and deadline, and a "Need ৳X more / overdue" footer. Every goal looks identical apart from its numbers.

Below are nine redraws. They differ in what shape carries the progress — a liquid level, an arc, a staircase, a stepped tile grid, a comet, a pair of gauges — because a bar labelled "0%" and a bar labelled "0%" are the same object no matter how prettily it is filled.

1Nothing distinguishes a goal. Two goals, one at 92% and one at 3%, are the same rectangle with a different number in it. The card does not LOOK like how the goal is doing.
2"0%" is a dead end. A goal with nothing saved yet shows an empty track — no signal at all that the goal is on fire. The pace of a goal is invisible until it is nearly late.
3"overdue" is the only warning. The screen has a whole pace model — ahead, on track, behind, overdue — but the card only surfaces the last one, as an orange word in the corner.
4The date is decoration. "by 27 Nov 2026" sits in the meta row at the same weight as everything else, even when it is the only thing that matters.
