Accounts as cards

The Accounts screen today renders each account as a soft wash card — a tone-tinted gradient with a 3px left accent strip, the account name, the balance, and a "Δ since opening" line. It reads as a tinted rectangle.

Below are six ways to make it read as an actual credit or bank card. They differ in how much of the card metaphor they commit to, and in how much they keep of the information the screen already carries.

1It does not read as a card. A wash + a left stripe is a tinted tile. There is no silhouette, no aspect ratio, nothing that says "held object".
2The card metaphor can lie. A real bank card has a printed number, a chip, a bank name. This app has none of those — so any version that draws them is inventing data the user never entered.
3The tone already carries meaning. Green = bank, gold = cash, blue = wallet, red = card. Whatever silhouette we choose, that colour language has to survive it.
