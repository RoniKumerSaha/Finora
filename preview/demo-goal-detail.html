Goal detail — the till receipt, torn and re-written

A goal is a receipt: plain paper, a dashed rule, a running total and a stamp. The detail screen keeps that grammar and adds the two things a receipt never had — a fill line (how close you are) and an edit pass where the name, target and date are re-written on the same slip. The contribution history hangs below as the itemised list of everything you've paid in.
