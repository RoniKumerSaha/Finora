Transactions — nine ways to read a month of money

The transaction list is the one screen where a card has to hold a hundred rows, so it is the one screen where the layout itself carries meaning. Below are nine alternatives. Every one uses the same palette, the same tokens and the same data — what changes is what the page asks you to read first: the running total, the day's story, the category, the amount, the month, or nothing at all.

T1 is the current design, kept as the baseline. T5 and T6 are the two that argue hardest for the summary; T3 and T9 are the two that argue for time. Each card carries a short note on what it buys and what it costs.
