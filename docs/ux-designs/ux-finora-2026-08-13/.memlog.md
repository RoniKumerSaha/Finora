### override — danger color too close to accent (2026-08-14)

- **type:** override
- **text:** User flagged via screenshot that the current danger (`#D67560`) reads as nearly the same color as the accent gold (`#D9B26B`) — both sit in the warm orange-red family. In the "Wipe all data" / "Danger zone" callout, the danger border, the danger text, and the danger button all collapse into one flat warm hue. The danger soft-fill (`rgba(214,117,96,0.15)`) over deep forest bg has insufficient contrast — the callout box barely reads as a warning. Resolution: needs a new danger role that is visually distinct from the accent gold AND has adequate contrast against the dark bg. Specifically: (a) hue must move away from the orange-red family on the color wheel, (b) danger callout needs a real surface treatment, not just a tinted transparent, (c) the heading, button, and border should differ in saturation/weight so they don't collapse.

### decision — variant C: louder red + real surface for both callouts
