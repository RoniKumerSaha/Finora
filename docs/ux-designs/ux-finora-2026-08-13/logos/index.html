About this logo

Three ascending bars — two violet (shorter, in the past), one teal (taller, current). It reads as "your money has been growing", which is the underlying promise of Finora: track where money came in and where it went out, and the result is a visible upward shape.

  • Concept: bars grow left-to-right, signaling growth without being literal "money".
  • Palette: matches the Midnight theme tokens — #2DD4BF primary, #A78BFA accent, #5C6670 muted.
  • Scale: works as a 16×16 favicon and as a 1024×1024 app icon — the simple shapes survive small sizes.
  • Readability: dark tile on light, light tile on dark; the brand background variant inverts the bars to dark.
