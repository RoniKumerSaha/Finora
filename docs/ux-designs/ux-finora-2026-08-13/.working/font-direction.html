Finora — font direction

Four candidates rendered in the same Finora surface (Home dashboard + Transactions list + Danger modal). Headings set in the serif; body remains system sans. Tabular numerals everywhere money appears.

F · Fraunces

Modern warm serif with optical sizing. Slightly characterful. Pairs naturally with the parchment palette. "Your money journal."

Playfair is the loudest option — the high stroke contrast and sharp triangular serifs feel "magazine-y" rather than "personal money journal." May clash with the warm-parchment palette's easygoing register.

S · Sans-only (baseline)

System sans for everything. No serif. Cleanest, most neutral. "Calm / trustworthy" leaning hardest.
