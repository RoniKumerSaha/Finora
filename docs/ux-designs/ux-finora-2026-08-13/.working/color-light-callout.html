Finora — light-mode callout surfaces

Three light-mode treatments for the danger callout and success alert. Same dark-mode rule (real surface, not transparent tint) — but the "real surface" needs to read as warm parchment-tinted, not gray. All three use the same #B8473A danger / #558C6C success hex; only the callout bg varies.

A · Warm cream tint

Closest analog to dark mode's #2A1A18 — a warm tint that sits on the parchment palette. bg #2A1A18 dark ↔ #F2E4DD light. Subtle but distinct from the warm cream page bg.

B · Purer white tint

Almost-white tints with just a hint of role hue. Crispens against the cream page bg. Feels closer to "clean card" than "tinted wash." Less warm, more clinical.

Pushes the tints further into the role hue. Reads as a louder warning. Good if you want the callout to feel "I'm stopping you" rather than "FYI."
