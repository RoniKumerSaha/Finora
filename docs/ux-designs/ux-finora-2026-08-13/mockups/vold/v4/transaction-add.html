Type · 8 modes per PRD §9.2
Tabs wrap on narrow viewports. Each type surfaces different source/destination accounts and ledger side-effects.
BDT
Transfer
Transfer does not affect Surplus or Net Worth — only moves liquid balance across accounts.
Savings / invest contribution
Reduces Available-to-Plan and grows the linked goal balance. Does not reduce Surplus.
Debt repayment
Lowers outstanding principal. Auto-creates a payment entry in the debt ledger.
Borrowed money
Increases Liability and destination account balance. Plan a repayment in the Monthly Plan to lower it.
Money lent
Decreases source account and creates a Receivable. Receivable contributes to Net Worth but not to liquid balance.
Loan repayment received
Adds to selected deposit account and lowers the corresponding receivable by the same amount.
