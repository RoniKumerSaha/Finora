Month Planner — four directions

Pick the one that feels best. Each option is illustrative (playful), pure-scratch, and would be built into the new /plan sidebar route. Same content, different metaphor.

Option A
🌊

Money River

Income above, expenses below, a savings runway between them. The shape of the month as a flowing river — feels meditative, more "vibe" than "spreadsheet".

Pie Donut

The classic "where each taka goes" donut, with the savings slice glowing green. Most familiar shape from budget apps — fast to read, easy to edit inline.

Option C
🏺

Piggy Jars

Each category is a glass jar — fill from the bottom, warn when overflowing. Most playful, most tactile. Empty jars go red. Best for users who think in objects, not numbers.

Open →
Option D
📅

Calendar Heatmap

The month is a real calendar grid — click any day, see what was planned. Heatmap (GitHub-style) shows heavy vs light days at a glance. Best for users who plan by date.

Option E · Hybrid
🏺🌊

Jars + River

Best of both. The jars are your categories (input: budget, planned spend, quick chips). The river is the flow readout (output: where each taka will move through the month). Edit a jar, the river reshapes.
