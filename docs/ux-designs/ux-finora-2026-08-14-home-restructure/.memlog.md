# Finora — Home & Insights restructure (2026-08-14)

## Run summary

User feedback: Home and Insights feel similar. Both pages show
"your money" content. Restructure to remove the duplication.

This run is an **Update** to the parent V2 design
(`ux-finora-2026-08-13/DESIGN.md` + `EXPERIENCE.md`) and to the
analytics spine (`ux-finora-2026-08-14-analytics/`). It does not
introduce new visual tokens.

## Decisions

### D1 — Two pages, two distinct jobs

**Home** is the at-a-glance snapshot. Always point-in-time. No date
control. The user opens Finora and sees their *current* balance, this
month's income, this month's expense, and a few things that need
attention right now.

**Insights** (`/insights`) is the analytical surface. Date range pills
stay here. Charts live here. List cards (goals/debts/investments) live
here. The user explicitly navigates here when they want to "look at
history."

Why: the brand promise is "observe, not act." Observation has two
modes — point-in-time (Home) and period-bounded (Insights). Mixing
them in one page forces a control that doesn't belong at the top of
the home screen.

### D2 — What stays on Home

Stat row (3-up): Total balance, Income (this month), Expense (this month).
Accounts preview (top 4, with balances).
Recent activity (top 4 transactions).
Onboarding callout (when empty).
A single "See full Insights →" affordance under the cards.

### D3 — What moves to Insights (or stays there)

Stat row (3-up): Net flow, Avg monthly expense, Saved toward goals.
Date range pills.
Cash flow chart (SVG).
Spending by category (top 6 + Other).
Net worth trajectory (line).
Goals card (5 rows).
Debts card (5 rows).
Investments card (5 rows).

These are period-bounded. None of them make sense without a date range.

### D4 — What is removed

- The "Debts" preview card on Home (3 rows + I owe / Owed to me summary).
  The user goes to Insights for that. Home can show a one-line summary
  if we want, but not a full card.
- The "Investments" preview card on Home (3 rows + total invested).
  Same reason.
- The full goals preview card on Home (3 rows with progress bars).
  Home can show a pill/line ("2 goals active — 1 needs attention") if
  we want, but not a full card.
- The "Recent activity" card — wait, this stays on Home. Recent
  transactions are point-in-time ("today's lunch"), not period-bounded.

### D5 — Date range pills live only on Insights

Home is fixed to "this month" implicitly. The user does not get a
date control on Home. If they want to see "last 6 months," they go to
Insights.

### D6 — Empty state on Home

When the user has zero transactions AND zero accounts AND zero goals
AND zero debts AND zero investments, Home shows a single onboarding
callout pointing to the three primary actions: "Add an account",
"Add a transaction", "Set a goal". No chart skeletons, no list
widgets, no goal/debt/investment empty states.

Once they have at least one account, the Home snapshot renders with
zeros — that's acceptable. The user has entered the app.

### D7 — "See full Insights" affordance

A single-line link at the bottom of the Home content, right-aligned:
"See full Insights →" navigates to `/insights`. Inherits the
transactions-list "Manage →" pattern. Not a giant button.

### D8 — The `◇` Insights nav item stays

The Insights nav item in the Shell sidebar is unchanged. It still
routes to `/insights`. The "See full Insights →" link from Home is a
secondary affordance, not a replacement for the nav.
